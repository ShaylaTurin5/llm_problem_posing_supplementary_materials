# Supplementary Materials: LLM Scaffolding for Mathematical Problem Posing

This repository contains supplementary materials for a research study comparing Socratic and Direct LLM scaffolding during mathematical problem posing.

## Contents

**LLM_prompt_scenario.txt**

This file includes:

- **Socratic Scaffolding Prompt:** Instructions for generating two guiding questions to support problem revision without providing revised formulations.
- **Direct Scaffolding Prompt:** Instructions for generating two alternative versions of a participant's mathematical problem.
- **Problem-Posing Scenarios:** The Handshake and Making Change scenarios used in the experimental sessions, along with a separate Food Drive scenario used during training.

Both scaffolding conditions use shared evaluation criteria involving conceptual accuracy, structural clarity, and cognitive demand, with Bloom's Taxonomy guiding the assessment of cognitive demand.

## Study Context

The study compared two forms of LLM-generated feedback during mathematical problem posing. Participants constructed mathematical problems, reviewed feedback, and revised their problems under both scaffolding conditions.

## Anonymous Review

These materials are provided to support methodological transparency and reproducibility during anonymous peer review.
